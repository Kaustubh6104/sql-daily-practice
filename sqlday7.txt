Q31. Find salespeople who have multiple customers. 
Ans : select s.sname from 
salespeople s join Customers c 
On c.snum = s.snum
Group by sname having count(c.snum) > 1;

Q32. Find salespeople with customers located in their city
Ans : select s.sname , c.cname, s.city from 
	Customers c join salespeople s 
	On c.city = s.city 

Q33.Find all salespeople whose name starts with ‘P’ and the fourth character is ‘l’. 
Ans : select * from SalesPeople 
	Where sname like “P__I”;

Q34. Extract the Salespeople table in the following order : SNUM, SNAME, COMMISSION, CITY.
Ans : select Snum,Sname,comm as Commission , City
	From SalesPeople;
	

	
Q35.Write a query that uses a subquery to obtain all orders for the customer named Cisneros. Assume you do not know his customer number. 
Ans:select O.num, O.amt  
	From Orders O 	
	Where O.Cnum=( select Cnum from Customers where 
	Cname = “Cisneros”);
