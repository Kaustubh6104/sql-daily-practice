Q16.  List the names and commissions of all salespeople in London. 
Ans : select sname , comm from salespeople 
	Where city = ‘London’;



Q17. List all the orders of salesperson Motika from the Orders table. 
Ans :  select * from orders o 
Where o.snum = (select snum from salespeople 
	Where sname = ‘Motika’)


Q18. Find all customers with orders on October 3. 
Ans :select cname from customers 
Where  cnum = (select cnum from orders 
	Where odate like ‘10/03/96’)

Q19.Give the sums of the amounts from the Orders table, grouped by date, eliminating all those dates where the SUM was not at least 2000.00 above the MAX amount. 
Ans : select sum(amt) , odate from 
	Orders group by date 
	Having sum(amt) > = max(amt) +2000;

Q20.Write a query that uses the EXISTS operator to extract all salespeople who have customers with a rating of 300. 
Ans : select * from salespeople s where
exists (select  1 from customers c
Where c.snum = s.snum
	And Where rating =300);
