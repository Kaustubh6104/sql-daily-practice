
Q46.Write a query that calculates the amount of the salesperson’s commission on each order by a customer with a rating above 100.00. 
Ans: select sname,(s.comm*o.amt) as commission from salespeople s join ordes o on 
s.snum = o.snum and orders o join customers c on . c.cnum= o.cnum where c.ratings>100;
Q47Count the customers with ratings above San Jose’s average. 
Ans: select count(*) from customers where rating > (select Avg(rating) from customers where city = “San Jose”);

Q48.Write a query that produces all pairs of salespeople with themselves as well as duplicate rows with the order reversed. (hard)
Ans : SELECT s.snum, m.snum
FROM Salespeople s
CROSS JOIN Salespeople m
ORDER BY s.snum, m.snum;

Q49.Find all salespeople that are located in either Barcelona or London.

Q50. Find all salespeople with only one customer.
